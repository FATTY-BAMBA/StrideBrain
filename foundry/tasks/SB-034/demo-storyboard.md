# SB-034 first-60-second storyboard

Status: storyboard approved for implementation; NOT a timed result. First slice implemented locally. Authenticated recording and first-time-user comprehension remain pending.

Persona: first-time interior designer or company owner. Context: controlled synthetic Horizon demo with an authorized Xinyi session. No architecture explanation. A valid existing comparison may be shown only when current/fresh; otherwise the person explicitly runs comparison and actual wait time counts.

## Exact requested journey

| Step | Target time | What the user sees/does | Value they should recognize | Existing capability / presentation work |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 0–4 s | Land on Home. | “This is for my work.” | Compact outcome heading; existing Home route. |
| 2 | 4–9 s | See current work and three working tasks. | “I know what I can do and where to start.” | New launchpad presentation over existing authorized reads/routes. |
| 3 | 9–13 s | Open Xinyi Flagship Renovation. | “This is the project I am handling.” | Existing authorized project entry. |
| 4 | 13–18 s | Read compact project context and current commercial labels. | “I can see what is happening.” | Existing workspace data; clearer hierarchy. |
| 5 | 18–23 s | Click Compare with past work. | “I can use our previous experience.” | Existing explicit comparison command/result route. |
| 6 | 23–28 s | See ranked historical projects and why they match. | “I do not have to search old jobs manually.” | Existing ranked comparisons; short summary before long cards. |
| 7 | 28–36 s | See imported-stone / lighting / quote signals. | “I know what needs a closer look.” | Existing typed findings and insufficiency statements; no recomputed or hard-coded values. |
| 8 | 36–45 s | Open exact supporting evidence/source; return. | “I can check where that came from.” | Existing evidence and return context. |
| 9 | 45–55 s | Prepare and explicitly create an internal review action. | “I can turn a concern into accountable next work.” | Existing bounded form, consent, idempotency and receipt. |
| 10 | 55–60 s | Return to Home / StrideBrain; see bounded company questions and Planned assistance. | “This connects knowledge to daily work; more assistance is planned.” | Existing routes with proposed task links and truthful planned cards. |

These are design time budgets, not evidence that ten steps already fit in 60 seconds. In a first-user session, never rush the person through evidence, uncertainty or consent to satisfy the clock. Measure the five comprehension answers at 60 seconds; if meaningful review/action confirmation needs longer, record actual duration and continue after the comprehension checkpoint. A guided presenter recording and an uncoached first-user trial are different evidence types. If the full ten-step sequence is a strict 60-second release condition, it remains unproven and requires an observed result; do not relabel a partial walkthrough as complete.

## Intended “wow” moments

- Recognize relevant previous projects immediately after asking the question.
- Understand a specific current-project difference, with a plain next review step.
- Open the exact source without searching folders or asking a colleague.
- See a confirmed action and a durable receipt tied to the evidence.

These moments are hypotheses to test. No claims about hours saved, approval quality or autonomous completion are supported yet.

## Failure and recovery beats

No active session: show truthful access guidance; do not surface project names or create a session invisibly. No comparison: present the real comparison action. Stale result: preserve the stale block and rerun affordance. Insufficient evidence: show the gap, never fabricated confidence/range. No actions: show the honest empty state. Planned agent: informational representation only. On modal close/back/locale navigation, keep the user in the same authorized context.

## Manual recording plan, after approval

Capture Home → Xinyi → comparison → signals → evidence/source → review → receipt → broader task surface in one primary locale, then a short bilingual parity segment. Use only synthetic data and an explicitly authorized temporary session. Avoid recording login tokens/private browser data. A usable recording is not a new QA certification. No recording or authenticated session was created in Phase 1.
