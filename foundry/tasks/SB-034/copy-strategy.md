# SB-034 copy strategy

Status: first-slice bilingual copy implemented. Suggested question wording was narrowed to real distinct destinations. New Taiwan Traditional Chinese copy still needs human review; historical SB-032 attestation does not approve these new strings.

## Voice

Use verbs, familiar project/business language, specific outcomes and questions. Put working actions before explanation. Keep technical derivation in evidence details, authority/provenance labels where they affect interpretation, and architecture in developer documentation. Never turn a financial signal into a recommendation to approve or an unsupported savings claim.

## Financial and evidence rules

Retain quoted price / forecast direct cost / reconciled actual cost / supplier offer as separate concepts. Keep currency, normalization basis, authority, version and freshness visible where they change the decision. Do not rename Human reviewed to Approved. Do not call historical supplier offers current pricing. Evidence coverage of 100% does not mean financial certainty, approval or confidence of 100%. Similarity score is not a probability.

Do not hard-code the phone screenshots' percentages: the user changed the selected comparison set, so values changed. Render current stored authorized findings. When evidence is insufficient, say what is missing and what cannot be concluded. Do not convert absent/restricted amounts into zero. Keep uncertainty adjacent to the relevant signal and preserve explicit action consequences and consent.
